Privacy and data handling

How this app treats doctor and patient information.

Stored in Neon (PostgreSQL)

  • Your mobile number and professional profile (name, registration, clinic).
  • Letter drafts while you are editing.
  • Letter history metadata (template type, patient name label, date, S3 file reference).
  • Audit events (sign-in, preview, finalize, download, delete) without full letter text.

Stored on AWS S3

  • Final PDF files only, encrypted at rest, accessed via short-lived download links.

Not stored after finalize

  • Full letter HTML is not kept once a PDF is saved. Only the PDF in S3 and summary fields in Neon.

Your choices

  • Delete finalized letters from history (removes the Neon record and the S3 file).
  • Sign out on shared devices when you finish.

Use this application only on trusted networks and devices. Contact your clinic administrator for retention policies required in your region.
